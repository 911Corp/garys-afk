# Third-party notices and asset register

Gary's AFK ships no image, video or audio file made by a third party. Every scene is drawn by code
at runtime, every sound is synthesised live with the Web Audio API, and the thumbnails and icons in
`public/assets/` are screenshots of the app's own output.

## Names

"Flying Toasters" from the catalogue brief was renamed **Flying Floppies**, with original sprites
(floppy disks and CRT monitors with wings), to avoid any confusion with an existing screensaver.
The DVD Survivor logo is an original NGMI / Gary's AFK wordmark; no DVD logo is used. The Arcade
Room games (Pongo, Blob Wars, Snek, Night Run, Bricker) are original mini-animations.
